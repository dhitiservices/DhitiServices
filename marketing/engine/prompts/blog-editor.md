# Step 1: critique against the rubric

Score each criterion from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent) with one specific note. Be honest; a 5 means nothing important could be improved.

1. **Specificity.** Real documents, fields, systems, deadlines, roles and numbers. No vague generalities a reader could find anywhere.
2. **Original insight.** The bold insight sentence reframes the problem. The method includes at least one non-obvious point (the step teams skip, the fix that backfires, the cause that looks like an error but is not).
3. **Scenario opening.** Opens inside a concrete, recognisable moment, not with a definition, statistic or thesis. Pays the scene off at the end.
4. **Structure and scannability.** Exactly 8 `##` sections in sentence case with plain, informative headings. Short paragraphs. Bold lead-ins, numbered routines and one blockquote artefact where they help. No walls of text, no listicle bloat.
5. **Evidence.** At least 3 distinct credible external https sources linked inline on descriptive anchor text, each supporting the exact claim next to it. No unsupported statistics. Illustrative figures labelled.
6. **SEO.** Primary keyword appears naturally in the title, within the first 100 words, and in at least one `##` heading. Secondary keywords used where natural. Description 130 to 155 characters and compelling.
7. **Internal links.** 2 or 3 links to existing Dhiti posts, written as `[anchor](../slug/)`, using only slugs from the provided list, placed where they genuinely help.
8. **Call to action.** The last section makes the case for one owner and a routine, mentions that the work suits a trained back-office team, and ends with the two-sentence Dhiti paragraph in the house form. Natural, specific, never pushy.
9. **Voice and hygiene.** Plain British/Indian English, calm and practical. No em dashes, no banned phrases or AI clichés, no hype, no exclamation marks, no filler, no repetition, no fabricated facts or Dhiti claims.
10. **Length.** 1,300 to 1,900 words of body text (ideal 1,400 to 1,800).

# Step 2: revise

Apply every fix needed to bring each criterion to 4 or 5. Rewrite sentences, tighten, cut and restructure as needed. Rules while editing:

- Keep exactly 8 `##` sections. Do not add an H1.
- Remove every em dash (—) by rewriting the sentence, not by swapping in a hyphen.
