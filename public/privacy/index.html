Privacy notice

This notice explains what information Dhiti Services receives through this website, why we use it and the choices available to visitors.

Information you choose to send

When you submit a business enquiry or training application, we receive the details entered in the form, which may include your name, organisation or location, email address, phone number and message. We use this information to respond to the request, assess the relevant opportunity and keep necessary business records.

Website analytics

With your permission, we use Google Analytics through Google Tag Manager to understand visits, traffic sources, pages viewed and important interactions such as successful enquiries. Analytics is optional. Advertising storage and ad personalisation are disabled in our current setup.

Cookies and local storage

We store your analytics choice in your browser so the website can remember it. Essential storage may be used for security and basic website functionality. You can change your choice at any time using the button below.

Service providers

Website forms are delivered using Web3Forms. Website measurement is provided by Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager only when permitted by your analytics choice. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices.

Retention and protection

We keep enquiry and application information only for as long as it is reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was submitted, operational follow-up, security and applicable record-keeping obligations. We take reasonable steps to limit access and protect the information we hold.

Your choices

You may ask us to review, correct or delete information you submitted, subject to applicable requirements. You may also reject or withdraw optional analytics through the cookie settings on this website.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email info@dhitiservices.com.
